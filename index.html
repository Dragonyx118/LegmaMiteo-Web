Built for Events
That Models Miss

Global models like ECMWF operate at 9km resolution with 1-hour time steps. Supercells develop in 20 minutes over 5km. That gap is exactly what LegmaMiteo fills.

⚡

Lightning Detection

AS3935 Franklin sensor detects lightning strikes up to 40km away, tracking distance and energy — the first signal of an approaching supercell.

Learn more →
📉

Pressure Monitoring

A drop of 3–5 hPa in under two hours is a reliable precursor to severe convective development. BMP580 tracks this in real time with 0.06 hPa resolution.

See live data →
🌬️

Wind Shear Analysis

Rapid changes in wind direction and speed at ground level are indirect signatures of the atmospheric instability that drives hail-producing thunderstorms.

😷

Air Quality

MOD-AIR measures PM1/PM2.5/PM10, CO₂ and VOC in real time — critical for post-event analysis after dust storms, wildfires or industrial accidents.

🔔

Instant Alerts

Telegram notifications fire the moment thresholds are crossed — pressure drop, lightning within 10km, PM2.5 above WHO limits — with zero delay.

🌐

Open Data API

Every measurement is publicly accessible in real time via REST API. No key required for reads. CC BY-NC 4.0 — free for research and education.
